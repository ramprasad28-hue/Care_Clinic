FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how Care_Clinic works — registration, booking, and what happens when plans change.

Getting started

How do I create a patient account?

Use the registration page with your email address and a password of your choosing. Everything you book is tied to that account.

How do I find the right doctor?

Browse by speciality or look through the full doctor list. Only active specialities and doctors currently accepting appointments are shown, so everyone you see can be booked.

Booking an appointment

How do I book?

Choose a speciality, pick a doctor, and select an open time from their weekly availability. Confirm the booking and you are done — no account is required just to see the schedule, but you sign in to confirm a visit.

Are the times shown genuine availability?

Yes. Every open time is generated directly from the doctor's own weekly schedule. A slot disappears the moment someone books it, so two patients can never take the same time.

What is my appointment reference?

When you book, the appointment gets a reference in the form CC-2026-000123. Keep it — it identifies your booking exactly. You can always find it again in your appointments list.

Can I book with more than one doctor?

Yes. You can book appointments with different doctors, or with the same doctor at different times. The only limit is that a single time slot belongs to one patient at a time.

Changing your plans

Can I cancel an appointment?

Yes — you can cancel yourself until 24 hours before the appointment starts. After that, changes go through the clinic instead. Cancelling never deletes your record: the booking history is kept, and the time immediately opens up for other patients.

Can I reschedule instead?

Yes, within the same 24-hour window. Pick any open slot as the replacement; your original booking stays exactly as it is unless the new one is confirmed successfully.

Your account

I forgot my password. What now?

Use the password reset page and enter the email address on your account. Follow the instructions you receive to choose a new password.

Important

Care_Clinic arranges routine appointments only. If this is a medical emergency, do not use this website — contact your local emergency services or emergency care provider immediately.
