Contact

Getting in touch

Appointments at Care_Clinic are arranged online. Here is how to get started, and where the clinic's own contact details will appear once they are published.

Everything happens online

Booking, rescheduling, and cancellation are all self-service, so the fastest route to a visit is the website itself.

  • Book an appointment

    Open the doctors list, pick a speciality, and choose an open time. No account is needed to browse, but you will need one to confirm a booking.

  • Create an account

    Patient registration takes an email address and a password.

  • Manage a booking

    Once you are signed in, your appointments list and dashboard let you view, reschedule, or cancel your visits.

  • Trouble signing in

    Reset your password using your registered email address.

Clinic contact details are not published yet. The clinic's address, phone number, support email, and opening hours will appear here as soon as they are provided and verified. They are deliberately left blank rather than filled with placeholder details.
